The BEST Items To Counter EVERY Hero

Build smarter by countering multiple heroes and entire team compositions at once

▶Watch the Full Video Guide on YouTube

📖 Guide Overview

Most Deadlock guides focus on countering heroes individually, breaking down matchups one by one. This approach is different: you'll learn to build smarter by using items that counter multiple heroes and entire team compositions at once.

Instead of memorizing what beats a single matchup, you'll understand which items give you the most value against the majority of teams you face. By the end of this guide, you won't just be surviving each hero—you'll be countering them all at once.

While Bebop is objectively the best character in the game, even he's not immune to smart counter-building. (Though Reactive Barrier does get mentioned suspiciously often in this guide... just saying.)

🛡️ Passive Items

Passive items are silent lifesavers that do the work for you. If you're often dying to chain CC or setups you can't react to, these are your best choice.

Reactive Barrier
1600 souls Tier 2
Automatically activates when you get hit by any form of crowd control, creating a barrier
Counters:
Bebop (Hook) Abrams (Shoulder Charge) Doorman (Cart Combo)
💡 Pro Tip: No-brainer to buy as early as the laning phase into teams with lots of CC. Insanely good against heroes that rely on locking you down before finishing the job.
Debuff Reducer
Reduces the duration of stuns, slows and even ultimates that apply CC effects
Counters:
Abrams (Stun) Holiday (Lasso) Moe (Ultimate)
💡 Pro Tip: Perfect if you're tired of being stunlocked or stuck in crowd control each fight. Builds into Debuff Remover, a fantastic active item.
Spirit Shielding
1600 souls Tier 2
Heavily increases your survivability against spirit damage
Counters:
Double Spirit Lanes Spirit-Heavy Comps
💡 Pro Tip: One of the best early game pickups if you're dealing with lots of spirit heroes.
Spirit Resilience & Bullet Resilience
Stat-based passives that let you fine-tune your defense based on what the enemy team is running
Counters:
Spirit/Weapon Heavy Teams Melee Heroes (Bullet Resist)
💡 Pro Tip: Not early game items. Buy once you have core damage items. Press Tab and see if the majority of enemy team has spirit or weapon damage, then buy accordingly. Note: Bullet Resilience is weaker than Spirit Resilience because Armor-Piercing Rounds can counter it (50% chance to ignore bullet resist). Consider Battle Vest (cheaper + fire rate) or Plated Armor (deflects bullets and on-hit effects).
Spellbreaker
6400 souls Tier 4
Provides insane spirit resist and debuff resist
Counters:
Grey Talon Vindicta (Snipes)
💡 Pro Tip: Best spirit resist option in the game. So effective it can make heroes like Grey Talon useless.

⚡ Active Items

While passive items keep you alive, active items turn fights in your favor. Parry-related items are included here since they require button presses.

Counterspell
Parry Active
Transforms your parry to protect you from damaging effects of enemy abilities and items. Time the parry just before an ability reaches you.
Counters:
Bebop (Hook, Bomb, Uppercut) Lash (Slam) Grey Talon (Ult) Holiday (Lasso) Paige (Ult) Calico (Bombs)
💡 Pro Tip: Almost every ability in the game becomes useless against someone who has mastered counterspell. One of the few items that can be bought every game on any hero. WARNING: Counterspelling Bebop's bomb puts it on cooldown but he still gains a stack!
Rebuttal
800 souls Tier 1
On successful parry: heal, return damage, and gain 30% bonus damage for 6 seconds
Counters:
Viscous (Puddle Punch) Any Hero
💡 Pro Tip: Whenever you land one parry, they're as good as dead. Can parry Puddle Punch from Viscous and return damage.
Knockdown
Drops an anvil on someone's head, knocking them down and stunning them. Prevents and cancels many ultimates.
Counters:
Bebop (Ult) Moe (Ult) Flying Characters Moe & Krill (Burrowing)
💡 Pro Tip: Absolute menace against flying characters or anyone mid-animation. Hit Moe and Krill with knockdown when they're constantly burrowing to secure kills.
Slowing Hex
Massively reduces enemy movement and silences movement-based abilities and items for 3+ seconds
Counters:
Mirage (Bats Escape) Abrams (Chase) Ivy (Ult)
💡 Pro Tip: You'll have impact with this item almost every game. Getting slowed is infuriating and makes you an easy pick. Alternative: Warpstone for escaping (teleport + bullet resist).
Disarming Hex
Disarms target for 4 seconds and reduces their bullet resist
Counters:
Weapon-Heavy Teams Gun Damage Builds
💡 Pro Tip: Lifesaver that turns fights in your favor when enemy team is mostly weapon builds. Use for escaping or killing.
Silence Glyph
Launches a projectile forward, suppressing everyone in its path
Counters:
Calico Lady Geist (Low HP Ult) Yamato (Low HP Ult)
💡 Pro Tip: Especially effective against Lady Geist and Yamato who use ults when reaching low health. Watch them scramble in panic!
Unstoppable & Curse
Unstoppable: deflects all CC for 5.5 seconds | Curse: silence, disarm, prevent item usage, and interrupt abilities
Counters:
CC-Heavy Teams Fed Enemies Dynamo (Ult)
💡 Pro Tip: Curse is the best item to buy against fed people. Can cancel Dynamo's team ult.
Debuff Remover
Purges all non-ultimate negative effects that apply to you
Counters:
Multiple Debuff Teams Slowing Hex Knockdown
💡 Pro Tip: Priority buy against teams with multiple debuffs. Makes annoying actives like Slowing Hex and Knockdown irrelevant. Upgraded from Debuff Reducer.

💉 Anti-Heal Items

Some heroes have so much lifesteal that anti-heal items are almost required. Essential against: Victor, Shiv, Billy, Yamato, Abrams, Calico, Kelvin, Mirage, and Lady Geist.

Heelsbane
1600 souls Tier 2
Spirit damage applies 35% healing reduction. Heals you for 275 on kills.
Counters:
Victor Shiv Billy Yamato Abrams Calico Kelvin Mirage Lady Geist
💡 Pro Tip: Fantastic early game option good on all heroes. Game-changing when struggling in lane against constant healing. Not only are you more likely to get kills, but you heal straight back up.
Toxic Bullet
3200 souls
Deals bleed damage (% of max health over time) and applies 30% healing reduction
Counters:
Moe & Krill Beefy Heroes
💡 Pro Tip: 1600 more souls than Heelsbane with 5% less healing reduction, BUT the bleed deals % max health damage making it great into tanks.
Inhibitor
6400 souls Tier 4
35% healing reduction + reduces target's outgoing damage by 35%
Counters:
High Damage + Healing Heroes
💡 Pro Tip: Fantastic when an enemy hero is pumping damage while healing. Dual-purpose shutdown.
Spirit Burn
Tier 4
Deals significant spirit damage with burn that has 70% healing reduction
Counters:
Mirage (Spirit Build) Spirit-Building Heroes
💡 Pro Tip: Double the Heelsbane healing reduction (70%!) with insane damage. Value every game against spirit-building heroes.
Crippling Headshot
Tier 4
On headshot: reduces target's bullet/spirit resist by 18% for 12 seconds. Applies 30% healing reduction.
Counters:
Victor (Fed) Weapon Builds
💡 Pro Tip: Go-to for shutting down a fed Victor. Shreds resistances while applying anti-heal.

🎯 Final Thoughts

By now you're not just memorizing what to build against each matchup—you understand how these items work and what might give you the most value each game. Remember to press Tab frequently to assess enemy team composition and adapt your build accordingly.

The key is building items that counter multiple threats at once rather than hyper-focusing on a single hero counter.
